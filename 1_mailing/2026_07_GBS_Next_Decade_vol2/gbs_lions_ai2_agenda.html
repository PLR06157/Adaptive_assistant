We are continuing our 2026 Adaptive GBS Lion's Talks series with a session focused on a shift that is already reshaping how GBS teams operate.

AI is changing how GBS teams work, automate, and collaborate with IT. Business teams now have easier access to AI-enabled solutions than ever before – and that changes the rules of the game for GBS leaders. Join us to discuss what to anticipate as this access expands, how to secure what your teams build, and how real use cases are already driving productivity while keeping control.

What's in it for you?

Technology barriers between GBS Operations and IT are rapidly falling. Understand what this means in practice – how GBS Ops teams should position themselves as business users gain direct access to AI tools, how collaboration with IT must evolve, and how to lead your organisation through this transition before it outpaces your governance.

When business teams can build with AI directly, securing what they create becomes a shared responsibility. We will discuss the security and governance frameworks that every GBS leader should have in place – covering how to protect data, manage risk, and ensure that solutions built with AI meet the standards your organisation requires.

Hear real examples of AI driving automation and productivity within GBS Operations teams. From concrete workflows to measurable outcomes, these use cases offer a practical lens on what is already working – and how you can bring those results back to your own organisation.

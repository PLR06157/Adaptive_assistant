Adaptive GBS Masters

I’d like to invite you to the next meeting in the Adaptive GBS Masters series on 16 April, this time dedicated specifically to Business Acumen in GBS.

Meeting format:

Each session focuses on one specific topic related to the GBS/SSC/BPO industry. We place strong emphasis on sharing experiences and perspectives among C-level leaders responsible for managing GBS centers and processes. The discussion is lightly moderated; the main idea is to enable open and free-flowing exchange. The group is intentionally limited to a dozen or so participants to ensure a smooth conversation and highly effective discussion. Participants are exclusively C-level experts. The meeting is scheduled for three-four hours.

Topic for this session:
We will discuss how GBS centers support companies in the Business Acumen in GBS. How should knowledge about a corporation’s products and services be embedded within GBS to support the organization strategicy? How can GBS employees learn about the corporation and its core business during onboarding? Are there “glass walls” between GBS and the business? Does the scope of processes handled in GBS - such as finance, procurement, HRO, marketing, and supply chain - help build business acumen? Are entrepreneurial competencies needed among employees of service centers?

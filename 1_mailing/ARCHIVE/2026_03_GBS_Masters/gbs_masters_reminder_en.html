Meeting format:

Each session focuses on one specific topic related to the GBS/SSC/BPO industry. We place strong emphasis on sharing experiences and perspectives among C‑level leaders responsible for managing GBS centers and processes. The discussion is lightly moderated; the main idea is to enable open and free‑flowing exchange. The group is intentionally limited to a dozen or so participants to ensure a smooth conversation and highly effective discussion. Participants are exclusively C-level experts. The meeting is scheduled for three hours.

Topic for this session:

We will discuss how GBS centers support companies in the Life-Sciences, Healthcare, Tech‑Med, and Pharma sectors, going far beyond traditional back‑office functions. We will focus on key areas such as:

  • regulatory affairs,
  • clinical trials operations,
  • quality,
  • data management and analytics,
  • specialized IT.

We will explore whether these services are delivered in a full GBS model, in a "functional‑led" approach, or perhaps in a hybrid setup. GBS organizations with experience in providing these functions will share practical insights — including how these capabilities were introduced, the challenges involved, and plans for expanding into additional areas.

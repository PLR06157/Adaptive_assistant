I would like to invite you to the next session of the Adaptive GBS Masters series, this time exceptionally dedicated to the Life Science industry. The topic of the upcoming meeting is Core Life-Science Related Functions in GBS.

First and foremost – the meeting format:

Each session focuses on a single, specific topic related to the GBS/SSC/BPO industry. We place particular emphasis on the exchange of experiences and insights from C-level leaders responsible for managing GBS centres and processes. The discussion is only lightly moderated; its main idea is a free and open exchange of views. The group is intentionally limited to a dozen or so participants to ensure the conversation flows smoothly and the discussion is as effective as possible. Participants are exclusively C-level experts. The meeting is planned for three hours.

We will discuss how GBS centres support companies from the Life Sciences, MedTech and Pharma sectors, going far beyond classic back‑office functions. We will focus on key areas such as:

  • regulatory support (regulatory affairs),
  • clinical operations (clinical trials),
  • quality (quality),
  • data management and analytics,
  • specialised IT.

We will consider whether such services are delivered in a full GBS model, a functional-led approach, or perhaps a hybrid model. GBS organisations with experience in delivering these processes will share practical observations: they will describe how these functions were implemented, the challenges involved, and plans for expanding into new areas.

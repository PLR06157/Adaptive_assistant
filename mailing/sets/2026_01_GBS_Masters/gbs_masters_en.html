First – meeting format:

At each meeting we discuss only one topic related to our GBS/SSC/BPO industry, with an emphasis on exchanging experiences and opinions from C-level people managing centers/processes in GBS. The discussion is lightly moderated, but we focus on open discussion. The meeting is set for three hours, which should be enough to explore the topic together and for each participant to share their opinions and ask all the questions they have on the subject. The group is limited to a dozen or so people to ensure the discussion is effective and efficient. Participants are exclusively C-level individuals.

Topic for this meeting:

Digital Transformation and AI: From hype to actual results.

AI has stopped being an experiment – it's already in our operations, whether we want it or not. The question is: are we controlling this change, or is it controlling us? During the discussion we will address uncomfortable questions:

  • Where does AI actually work? Market examples – what worked, what failed.
  • How to talk to HQ about AI budgets when ROI is uncertain?
  • Can we afford to "wait until others test it" – and what do those who wait lose?

Come with questions, leave with concrete takeaways – based on the experiences of leaders who have already been through it.

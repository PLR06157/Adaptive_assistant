This time, Adaptive is heading to Kraków for an engaging session co-hosted with Sappi:

AI in GBS world: your Friend or Foe?

We will explore AI use cases in GBS, from the positive and practical to the negative and risky.

Learn how attackers misuse AI, how it might be used in a GBS environment, and why AI is both a security challenge and a cybersecurity enabler.

What's in it for you?

Gain first-hand insights from industry leaders who are already leveraging AI in their GBS operations. Learn how to navigate barriers to adoption, manage transformation effectively, and identify real-world opportunities for automation and efficiency. Plus, connect with peers and decision-makers during an evening of open discussion, exchange, and networking in a relaxed atmosphere.

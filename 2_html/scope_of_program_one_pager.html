Phase 1

Diagnostic, Design & Pilot Deployment

step 01

Mobilize & Diagnose

Understand the current reality

Establish the program structure and create a fact base on how work is performed today across the fragmented European OTC landscape.

  • Mobilization & program setup — delivery structure, working model, and stakeholder alignment.
  • Current-state diagnostic — fragmentation, pain points, data conditions, and local variation.
  • Pilot scope & country definition — select the right first deployment boundaries.
  • Process & operating model assessment — identify standardization opportunities.
step 02

Design the Target Model

Define how the future state will work

Translate transformation intent into a clear operating model, governance structure, and design authority for the pilot scope.

  • Future-state OTC design — target process model, control points, and ways of working.
  • Business requirements & design authority — clear requirements and consistent decisions.
  • Governance & decision-making setup — accountability, escalation, and management structure.
  • Change impact & stakeholder alignment — prepare business teams for adoption.
step 03

Prepare, Validate & Deploy

Convert design into a live proof point

Ensure the business is ready, validate the solution from a process perspective, and deploy the initial pilot.

  • Pilot readiness planning — deployment conditions and operational readiness before go-live.
  • Testing coordination & business validation — ensure the solution is fit for production use.
  • Pilot deployment — Cash Application and Collections go live.

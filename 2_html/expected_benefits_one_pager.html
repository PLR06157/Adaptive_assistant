The control benefit comes from standardizing work allocation, workflows, dashboards, and reason-code discipline across a highly fragmented landscape.

Working capital

  • Reduce unmatched payments from ~18,000/month to ~7,500/month at 90% auto-match.
  • Reduce unapplied cash backlog from the current 5,246-item starting point.
  • Resolve ~300 disputes/month faster to reduce blocked cash.
  • Support DSO improvement through predictive and automated collections logic.

Productivity & efficiency

  • Every 1-point match-rate gain removes ~750 items/month from manual handling.
  • 90% auto-match removes ~10.5k exceptions/month versus today.
  • 95% auto-match removes ~14.25k exceptions/month versus today.
  • 15%–20% productivity opportunity across 97 FTE implies ~14–19 FTE capacity release.

Control & management

  • Standardize work allocation across 25 countries, 8 languages, and 30 currencies.
  • Strengthen governance across ~75 ERP instances and ~25 banks.
  • Improve KPI visibility through dashboards, exception tracking, and harmonized workflows.
  • Reduce local process variation through common taxonomies and reason codes.

What can be quantified now

  • Auto-match uplift: 76% current baseline versus 90%+ target potential.
  • Manual exception reduction: ~10.5k fewer items/month at 90%; ~14.25k fewer at 95%.
  • FTE productivity: 15%–20% directional opportunity across 97 FTE.
  • Operational volumes: ~75,000 incoming payments/month, ~250,000 invoices/month, ~300 disputes/month.

What remains directional

  • DSO cash-value impact until receivables value and DSO baseline are confirmed.
  • Credit value until active customer count and credit volumes are validated.
  • Order-management value until order volumes are confirmed.
  • Final realized savings until pilot validation confirms process, data, and policy assumptions.

"The technology is ready.
The organisations are not."

    

      
Today
Acknowledge the Shift
  • Recognise AI-assisted development as a real, present trend
  • Start controlled pilots – small, low-risk, measurable
  • Map which IT roles are already under pressure
  • Begin internal dialogue between IT and business

      
6–12 Months
Build the Governance
  • Establish governance for AI-built apps
  • Upskill IT towards DevOps and CyberSec
  • Train business users on responsible AI usage
  • Define what business can build, what IT validates

      
1–3 Years
New Normal
  • Business-led development is the standard model
  • IT operates as a platform team, not a dev shop
  • Governance and security are IT's primary value
  • OCM competencies become the differentiator

    

    
Preparing means acting before the pressure forces you to. GBS Ops and IT teams that align now – on governance, skills, and platform – will lead the next decade. Those who treat this as someone else's problem will follow.

Commercial logic

Pricing has three components: subscription pricing, support pricing, and implementation. Subscription fees are billed annually for the base transaction limits, overages are billed monthly, and support is billed at the start of each subscription year.

  • Transaction definition covers invoices, memos, cash receipts, bank lines, lockbox items, credit applications, credit reports, and related source transactions.
  • Subscription starts at the beginning of the implementation project.
  • Support covers L1, L2, and L3 support.
  • Implementation fees are billed in equal monthly installments per the statement of work.
  • Payment terms: Net 30 from billing date.
  • Any extended implementation time is billed accordingly.

Implementation baseline

The source scope describes a Europe-wide rollout context with 25 countries, 160-170 bank accounts in 25 banks, 30 currencies, Oracle ERP, and multilingual correspondence requirements.

  • Languages: English, German, French, Polish, Swedish, Dutch, Spanish, and Italian.
  • Monthly volume context: 250,000 invoices and 75,000 payments.
  • Cash application scope includes payment mix detail, 300 disputes, 97 FTEs, 75% current match rate, and 5,246 current unapplied cash.
  • Collections / dispute / deduction / reporting uses the same regional and operational baseline.
  • E-Invoicing & EIPP keeps the same regional footprint and references Gia Pay at 30 cents per payment for online payments to one bank.

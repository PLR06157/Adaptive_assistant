OTC Transformation | Delivery Approach and Timeline

A pilot-first delivery model that proves value early and scales only after validated outcomes

The program is designed as a phased transformation rather than a big-bang rollout across the full OTC landscape. The delivery model is intended to balance speed, control, and scalability in a highly fragmented multi-country environment.

Why this timeline reduces risk

Decision quality improves stage by stage

Avoid a big-bang deployment

What this model creates

  • Clear separation between business foundation work and implementation so the pilot starts from a stronger operating baseline.
  • Controlled overlap between Cash Application and Collections pilots to balance early value with manageable complexity.
  • Explicit stage gates between pilot success, scale-up preparation, and broader enterprise rollout.
  • A functional rollout order that allows each wave to benefit from prior learning instead of restarting design assumptions.
